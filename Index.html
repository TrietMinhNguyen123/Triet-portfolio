About me

Triet is born in Vietnam, he is an immigrant that settled in the U.S. just a little bit before a Covid pandemic. The consequences of that is he is isolated and doesn't get a chance to talk and communicate, which in other words means he has a language barrier and it prevents him from connecting to other people. He is studious, hard-working as well as curious. Lastly, he is in a journey to exploit this world, he want to search and explore who he is and with all of his ambition and dream, he is stepping to make something big in his journey.
